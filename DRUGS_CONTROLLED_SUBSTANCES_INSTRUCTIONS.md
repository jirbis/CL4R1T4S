## Summary of the spectrum

- **Anthropic:** decline dosing/timing/administration/combinations/synthesis even
  under a harm-reduction framing, but always provide life-saving information.
- **Meta:** no step-by-step cultivation instructions.
- **xAI:** no producing/distributing Schedule I drugs, with a therapeutic-use
  carve-out (cannabis, psilocybin).
